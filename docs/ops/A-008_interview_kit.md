# User Research Interview Kit & Findings (A-008)

> **Scope**: 3 qualitative user research interviews (20 minutes each) conducted with residents of Delhi-NCR to evaluate real decision-making needs around air quality forecasts.
> **Privacy Rule**: All notes are anonymized. No personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifying details are stored in this document.

---

## 2. Pre-Established Decision Rules

These decision criteria are committed *prior* to conducting the interviews to avoid confirmation bias:

1. **Usage Frequency Threshold**:
   - *Rule*: If none of the 3 participants would open an honest air quality forecast app at least weekly during the winter smog season, revisit the mobile app scope and explore alternative delivery formats (e.g., static RSS/web widget or automated morning notification only).
2. **Actionable Forecast Threshold**:
   - *Rule*: If at least 2 of the 3 participants identify a concrete daily decision that a reliable 24- to 48-hour forecast would influence (e.g., outdoor exercise timing, school commute routing, masking, indoor air purifier scheduling), proceed with D+1 / D+2 forecast delivery. If fewer than 2 see a decision impact, refocus app copy on advisory guidance rather than raw metrics.
3. **Language Localization Threshold**:
   - *Rule*: If at least 2 of the 3 participants indicate a preference for Hindi or bilingual presentation over English-only, prioritize Hindi UI localization in Phase 2 before general release.
4. **Trust & Verification Criterion**:
   - *Rule*: If participants identify baseline comparison (historical station accuracy or comparison with persistence) as a decisive factor in trusting a forecast, make the "comparison against simple baseline" chart prominent on the main forecast view.

---

## 3. The 8 Open Non-Leading Questions

1. **Current Information Sources**:  
   *What do you currently check to know the air quality in your area?*
2. **Decision Impact**:  
   *What specific decision or daily action would a reliable 1- to 2-day forecast change for you?*
3. **Behavior on Bad Air Days**:  
   *What do you typically do on a 'Poor' or 'Very Poor' air quality day?*
4. **Trust and Credibility**:  
   *How would you decide whether to trust a new air quality forecast?*
5. **Language Preference**:  
   *Do you prefer reading forecasts and health advisories in Hindi, English, or both?*
6. **Expected Frequency of Use**:  
   *How often would you realistically open an air quality forecast app during winter versus summer?*
7. **Churn & Uninstall Triggers**:  
   *What would make you stop using an air quality app or delete it from your phone?*
8. **Handling Discrepancies**:  
   *How do you currently react when different air quality apps give conflicting numbers?*
